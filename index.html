LivebBoard

A live dashboard for Blackboard Ultra. It finds every deadline, even where instructors skip the calendar, shows your grades and attendance, builds a study plan, and tells you the moment something changes.

It runs on your own computer. Your Blackboard data stays there: it is not sent to us.

Get it: search for LivebBoard in the Microsoft Store on Windows 10 or 11. It is a monthly subscription, billed by Microsoft, and you can try the demo first without an account.
